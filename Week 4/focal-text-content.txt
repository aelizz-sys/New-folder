THE FOCAL METHOD
====================================================================

Eyebrow:  HOW IT WORKS
Heading:  The FOCAL Method

Intro:
Four weeks from first call to final delivery. No deck, no discovery
phase, no invoice surprises.

--- 01 ---
Title:  Listen
Text:   A ninety-minute call. What are you actually selling, and to whom.
        We take notes, you do most of the talking.
Pill:   WEEK ONE

--- 02 ---
Title:  Frame
Text:   Treatment, references, shot list, budget. You approve every line
        before anybody books a van.
Pill:   WEEK TWO

--- 03 ---
Title:  Make
Text:   One to three shoot days. You are welcome on set, encouraged to
        bring snacks, and asked to bring one decision-maker rather than
        a committee.
Pill:   WEEK THREE

--- 04 ---
Title:  Finish
Text:   Selects, retouch, grade, delivery in every format you need. Two
        revision rounds included, a third if we got something wrong.
Pill:   WEEK FOUR
