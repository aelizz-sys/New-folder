ABOUT ONEWE

Five members, One mind

ONEWE (원위) is a 5-member South Korean band under RBW, consiting of Yonghoon, Harin, Kanghyun, Dongmyeong, and Giuk. The group was originally known as MAS 0094 and debuted under Modern Music in August 2015. The band moved to RBW in June 2017 under the name MAS. The members officially re-debuted with the name ONEWE under RBW Entertainment on May 13, 2019.
