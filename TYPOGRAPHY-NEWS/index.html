Downtown Library Opens a 24-Hour Digital Lab for Students

The new space offers free design software, fast computers, and late-night study support for students across the city.

By maria lopez | September 25, 2026 | 5 min read

Starting this Monday, students will have round-the-clock access to a brand-new digital lab on the second floor of the Downtown Public Library, complete with 40 computers, drawing tablets, and a quiet recording booth.

The lab is the result of a two-year partnership between the library and three local colleges. Library staff say the goal is simple: give every student the tools they need to create, no matter their budget.

"Many of our students work during the day and study at night," said Dr. Hannah Reyes, the library's director. "We wanted a space that works on their schedule, not ours."

What's Inside the Lab

Each workstation comes loaded with professional software for web design, photo editing, and video production. Here is what students can use:

  • 40 computers with dual monitors
  • 12 drawing tablets for digital illustration
  • A soundproof booth for podcasts and voice-overs
  • Free printing for up to 20 pages per day

How to Book a Workstation

Students can reserve a computer for up to three hours at a time. Reservations open every morning at 8:00 a.m. on the library website at library.example.org/digital-lab.

Quick Tips for First-Time Visitors

Bring your student ID, save your work to a USB drive or cloud account, and arrive ten minutes early for your first session so staff can help you log in.

"This lab gave me the tools to build my first real website. I didn't have to buy anything."

Jamal Carter, first-year design student

What Comes Next

The library plans to add free weekend workshops on web design, typography, and coding starting in October. Workshop topics will be chosen by student vote, and every session will be open to beginners.

"We see this lab as a starting point," Reyes said. "If students keep showing up, we will keep growing it."
