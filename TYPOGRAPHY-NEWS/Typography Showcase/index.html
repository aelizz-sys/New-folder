Everyday Cloud Bag

Baggu makes the Everyday Cloud Bag for your everyday lifestyle

The Everday Cloud bag from BAGGU is an everyday-sized version of the Small Cloud Carry-On with all the lightweight functionality of the original.

By abygail elizondo | September 27, 2026 | 3 min read

Known for their simple designs with a playful attitude, BAGGU crafts for the everyday lifestyle.

Inspired by the Small Cloud Carry-on, the Everday Cloud Bag sizes down to fit the things that make you happy, everyday.

Baggu takes pride in their sustainable craftsmanship. "All of our heavyweight nylon styles are made with recycled nylon filament yarn, produced from pre-consumer waste," as stated on the materials section of the product. "This saves scrap material from being landfilled, conserves petroleum resources and reduces greenhouse gas emissions."

Features of the Everday Cloud Bag

Features a tech pocket that fits up to a 13/14” laptop, interior snap pocket, exterior front and side pockets, and packs into itself for easy storage plus more:

  • Interior snap pocket
  • In-pocket key leash
  • Four exterior slip pockets
  • Padded shoulder straps
  • Heavyweight nylon (59% recycled)
  • 10" H x 13.8" W X 6" L
  • Interior Tech Pocket Maximum Device Dimensions: 12.3" H x 9" W x 0.75" L
  • Strap/Handle Drop Length: 13.5"
  • Machine wash cold

The color palette of the Everyday Cloud Bag

The Everyday Cloud bag is available in two beloved prints such as black and cream gingham, and leopard. For the simple yet stylish, you can choose from solid colors like cherry, caper, fig, brown, and black.

The Everyday Cloud bag is designed to fit the essentials and more

The Everyday Cloud Bag is designed to let you overpack in peace. Along with Baggu's own 13/14" Puffy Laptop Sleeve, Go Pouch Set-Medium Pouch, and Go Pouch-Small Pouch, you can add your phone, 32 oz water bottle and keys.
