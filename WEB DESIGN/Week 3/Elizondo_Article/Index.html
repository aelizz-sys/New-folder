Aerith Gainsborough

Aerith Gainsborough, alternatively known as Aeris, is a major recurring character in the Final Fantasy VII series. She is the last of the ancient race, the Cetra, who possess powerful magical abilities. As a result, the Shinra Electric Power Company has hunted her throughout her life, seeking to exploit her powers. In Midgar, she meets Cloud Strife and the two grow close as she joins his quest to fight Shinra and hunt down Sephiroth. Aerith is a playful, joyful, and carefree individual who is flirtatious with Cloud and compassionate with others.

Introduction & Background

  • She was born to Ifalna and Professor Gast Faremis in Icicle Inn on February 7th, 1985, making her half-human and half-Cetra. After Professor Hojo killed Gast and captured Aerith and her mother, they became subjects to experiments at the hands of Hojo and Shinra. When Aerith began to exhibit visions because of her growing powers, she drew what their caretaker, Geddie, interpreted as the promised land; a land Shinra believed was fertile with Mako energy.
  • Knowing that Aerith inherited powers that could be an alternative test subject, Hojo employed harsher testing on Ifalna. Another scientist, Faz Hicks, helped the women escape the building allowing for Ifalna to take Aerith to the Sector 5 slums church. They made it to the Sector 5 slums where Ifalna collapsed at the undercity station and passed away. Aerith, while looking for a doctor, found Elmyra Gainsborough, who eventually adopted her.

Aerith Gainsborough's Skills and Abilities

  • In game, Aerith wields staves in battle and is defaulted as a back row magic user equipped from Materia. Her Limit abilities are defensive, either providing restorative powers, healing other party members, or bestowing status enhancements. Her ultimate weapon is the Princess Guard while her Ultimate Limit Break is the Great Gospel.
