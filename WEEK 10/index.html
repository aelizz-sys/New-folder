Made for every viewer

Every film at First Cut has open captions, so nobody has to ask for them. Saturday's 2:00 PM show is a relaxed screening: the lights stay low but on, the sound is softer, and you can move, talk or take a break whenever you need.

  • Open captions on every film
  • Printed transcripts at Podcast live
  • Relaxed screening: Sat, Dec 5, 2:00 PM
  • Step-free entrance and wheelchair spaces
