Started by Two Night-Shift Students

Night Owl began in 2019, when two graduate students got tired of studying in parking lots after the library closed. They rented a tiny room near campus, bought a used espresso machine, and stayed open until 2 a.m.

Seven years later, the room is bigger, the machine is new, and the promise is the same: good coffee and a calm place to think, long after everywhere else has closed.

A Room Built for Focus

The back room has 24 seats, an outlet at every table, and warm desk lamps instead of bright ceiling lights. Phone calls happen out front, so the back stays quiet.

During finals week we add extra tables, open an hour later, and keep a free snack basket by the door. You bring the notes, we bring the caffeine.
