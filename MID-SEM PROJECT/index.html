The Rot & Read book club is booked for October!

Turn the page with Rot & Read and delve into their monthly read and its book-loving community.

By abygail elizondo | October 19, 2026 | 3 min read

The Rot & Read book club was founded by social media influencer and book lover, Mellovesreading. Through platforms like TikTok and YouTube, she shares her love of reading with literary and lifestyle focused content.

As a university student and full-time content creator, she prioritizes productiity while maintaing a slow lifestyle to creae space for the little things they enjoy.

One of those hobbies is, of course, reading, which she dedicates her platforms to. When she's not sharing her trips to the library or her latest annotation guides for her current reads, Mel also hosts a book club on Fable.

Rot & Read book club on Fable!

If you have not heard of Fable, it is an app where users can log books and socialize with others about them through reviews or posts. While Mel logs books she reads on her own time, she also has a club with her followers where they choose a book to read each month. The past three months have included books like:

  • October: The Poisoner by I.V. Ophelia
  • September: The Knave and the Moon by Rachel Gillig
  • August: The Knight and the Moth by Rachel Gillig

The Rot & Read book club brings people together!

One of the most notable aspects about the Rot & Read book club is how people share their interest in reading. Whether the book of the month is completely new to a user or has been sitting on the "To be read" list, it is a safe space to exchange fun, like-minded dialouge.

"I found this club through Mel's YouTube channel and decided to step out of my comfort zone and finally try out a book club!"

Betzaida, comment from Rot & Read book club on Fable
